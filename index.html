Hello, I’m Benhur. I am a Researcher in Cybersecurity and AI at Rochester Institute of Technology, Dubai. Previously, I worked as a research assistant at Khalifa University for about a year, where I focused on developing novel routing protocols for ad-hoc mesh networks. I have also earned several industry-recognized cybersecurity certifications.

My interest lies in securing systems and networks using applicable techniques, including artificial intelligence and machine learning. I have analyzed malicious software targeting Linux and Windows, so malware analysis and detection is one of the areas I want to explore in my graduate studies. I would also like to delve into IoT security. Let me know if you have security-focused projects, and I will be happy to work on them.
